# Feedback

How to give us your feedback on the iMicroSeq specification (**Part D** of the testing instructions).

## Required: the feedback form

Complete the online [**feedback form**](https://forms.gle/tQaS8h7wcqxSaghE6) and click **Submit**. It is the only thing we need back from you. Nothing needs to be emailed.

The form covers: about you; privacy and data sharing; effort; fitness for purpose; gaps in the specification; completing Parts B and C; and adoption.

## Optional: the field-level feedback workbook

`iMicroSeq_Field-Level-Feedback_v1.2.xlsx` is for testers who want to give detailed, field-by-field comments. Use only the sheets that apply:

| Sheet | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Field feedback | Comments on individual fields. All 155 fields are pre-listed. |
| Missing fields | Information you hold that has nowhere to go in the specification. |
| Missing terms | Values you needed that were not in a pick list. |
| Scenario comparison | Differences between your Part B curation and the answer keys. |
| Validation issues | Validation messages that seemed wrong or unclear. |

If you complete it, email it to Emma Griffiths (emma_griffiths@sfu.ca), named `<organisation>_fieldfeedback_<YYYY-MM-DD>.xlsx`.

**Please don't send us your own data.** The file you curate in Part C stays with you.
